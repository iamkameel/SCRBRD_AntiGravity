# SCRBRD Rewards Engine

## Performance-Based Rewards & Sponsored Incentive Ecosystem

**Status:** Product Framework\
**Platform:** SCRBRD / CricketOS\
**Module:** Rewards\
**Purpose:** Convert verified cricket performance, contribution and
development into points, recognition and controlled sponsor rewards.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Overview

SCRBRD Rewards is a points-based incentive system that converts verified
cricket performance into a structured reward economy.

Players earn points for measurable cricket achievements including runs,
wickets, boundaries, milestones, strike rates, economy, catches,
stumpings, run-outs, Player of the Match awards and other performance
indicators. Points accumulate in a player's SCRBRD Rewards Wallet and
can be used to unlock sponsored discounts, vouchers, development
opportunities, experiences and prestige rewards.

The system must not simply reward raw statistical volume. A naïve model
would encourage stat-padding and could unfairly favour players who play
more matches, face weaker opposition or occupy roles that naturally
produce more visible statistics.

SCRBRD therefore combines:

1.  **Event Points** --- points generated by measurable cricket actions.
2.  **Performance Multipliers** --- adjustments for efficiency, quality
    and match context.
3.  **Integrity Controls** --- verification, thresholds and anti-gaming
    protections.
4.  **Reward Wallet** --- controlled accumulation and redemption.
5.  **Sponsor Marketplace** --- offers funded or supplied by approved
    partners.
6.  **Recognition System** --- tiers, badges, milestones and
    leaderboards.

The objective is to reward **performance, impact, consistency and
development**, rather than statistical volume alone.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Design Principles

The Rewards Engine should follow the following principles:

-   Cricket performance remains the primary source of points.
-   Only verified data should qualify for full reward value.
-   Match format and context must influence efficiency metrics.
-   Derived statistics such as averages should not be repeatedly
    double-counted.
-   Batters, bowlers, wicketkeepers, fielders and all-rounders must have
    meaningful earning opportunities.
-   Players should not be incentivised to play selfish cricket merely to
    maximise points.
-   Sponsor expenditure must remain commercially controllable.
-   Reward balances must not be treated as cash.
-   Reward rules should be versioned and auditable.
-   Minor-player privacy and POPIA controls must remain separate from
    advertising and sponsorship targeting.
-   Sponsored offers must never require protected player data to be
    exposed to sponsors.

## 3. Reward Engine Architecture

### Layer 1 --- Event Points

Base points are generated directly from verified scoring events and
match results.

Examples:

-   run scored
-   wicket taken
-   four
-   six
-   catch
-   stumping
-   run-out
-   maiden
-   milestone
-   Player of the Match

### Layer 2 --- Performance Multipliers

The engine can adjust qualifying points according to:

-   match format
-   opposition strength
-   competition level
-   match importance
-   innings situation
-   strike rate relative to format
-   bowling economy relative to format
-   pressure situations
-   verification status

### Layer 3 --- Integrity Controls

Controls protect the reward economy from manipulation.

Examples:

-   minimum balls faced
-   minimum overs bowled
-   minimum matches for aggregate awards
-   verified fixtures
-   scorer confirmation
-   anomaly detection
-   reward caps
-   duplicate-event protection
-   immutable transaction history

### Layer 4 --- Reward Wallet

Points are credited to the player's wallet only after the underlying
performance data satisfies the applicable verification rules.

# 4. Base Batting Points

  Event                          Base Points
  ---------------------------- -------------
  Run scored                               1
  Four                              +2 bonus
  Six                               +4 bonus
  30+ score                               +5
  Half-century                           +15
  Century                                +35
  150+                                   +60
  Meaningful not-out innings              +5

A milestone bonus is additional to points earned for the runs
themselves.

### Strike-Rate Bonuses

Strike-rate thresholds must be **format specific**. A single universal
strike-rate table would unfairly compare T20, limited-overs and
longer-format cricket.

An illustrative limited-overs model:

  Strike Rate     Bonus
  ------------- -------
  80--99             +5
  100--119          +10
  120--149          +18
  150+              +25

The production algorithm should compare strike rate against the relevant
format, competition and minimum-balls threshold rather than relying
solely on fixed bands.

# 5. Bowling Points

  Event                           Base Points
  ----------------------------- -------------
  Wicket                                   20
  Bowled / LBW wicket                      +5
  Maiden over                              +8

  5-wicket haul                           +30
  Hat-trick                               +50

Economy bonuses should only activate once a minimum number of legal

# 9. Context Multipliers

Context multipliers allow SCRBRD to recognise that identical raw
statistics can have different sporting value.

## Match Importance

Illustrative values:

  Match Type             Multiplier
  -------------------- ------------
  Friendly                     0.90
  League                       1.00
  Recognised rivalry           1.10
  Semi-final                   1.20
  Final                        1.30

These values should be configurable by competition rather than
hard-coded globally.

## Opposition Strength

  Opposition Context                  Multiplier
  --------------------------------- ------------
  Materially weaker level                   0.90
  Comparable level                          1.00
  Higher-ranked/top-tier opponent           1.15

Opposition multipliers require a defensible ranking system. Until SCRBRD
has sufficient data, this component should be conservative to avoid
circular rankings.

## Match Situation

Potential contextual modifiers include:

  Situation                     Example Multiplier
  --------------------------- --------------------
  Collapse recovery                           1.10
  Successful pressure chase                   1.10
  Powerplay bowling                           1.05
  Death-over bowling                          1.10

Context bonuses should be algorithmically defined wherever possible
rather than manually awarded.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. Illustrative Reward Formula

A conceptual formula is:

`Reward Score = Base Event Points × Context Multiplier × Quality Multiplier × Integrity Multiplier`

The exact production calculation should be version-controlled.

Every reward transaction should retain:

-   original event
-   rule version
-   base points
-   applicable multipliers
-   final points
-   fixture
-   player
-   verification state
-   timestamp

This allows SCRBRD to explain **why a player received a particular
score** and to reconstruct historical balances.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 11. Integrity and Verification

A points economy becomes vulnerable once rewards have real-world value.

SCRBRD should therefore implement multiple verification states.

  Verification State               Illustrative Multiplier
  ------------------------------ -------------------------
  Unverified/imported               0.70 or non-redeemable
  Verified scorer                                     1.00
  Coach/official confirmation                         1.02
  Official competition fixture                        1.05

The precise multipliers should be tested before launch. Verification
should primarily establish **eligibility and trust**, not create large
sporting advantages.

### Anti-Abuse Controls

The system should support:

-   minimum balls faced for strike-rate rewards
-   minimum deliveries for economy rewards
-   minimum innings/matches for averages
-   verified fixture requirement
-   duplicate event prevention
-   suspicious statistical spike detection
-   impossible-event detection
-   manual review workflow
-   configurable earning caps
-   redemption thresholds
-   transaction reversals rather than destructive deletion
-   audit logging

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. Derived Statistics

Averages and strike rates are derived from underlying events. Rewarding
them after every fixture risks double-counting the same performance.

They should instead drive **periodic bonuses**.

Examples:

  Period Metric                                  Example Bonus
  -------------------------------------------- ---------------
  Batting average above qualifying threshold               +25
  Bowling average below qualifying threshold               +25
  Division-leading strike rate                             +40
  Division-leading economy                                 +40
  Consistency award                               configurable

Qualification should require a minimum number of innings, balls, overs
or matches.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 13. Four Reward Families

SCRBRD should internally distinguish between different sources of reward
value.

## Performance Points

Direct sporting output:

-   runs
-   wickets
-   catches
-   boundaries
-   dismissals
-   milestones

## Impact Points

Contribution relative to match context:

-   pressure innings
-   decisive spell
-   successful chase
-   key partnership
-   high-value wicket
-   match-defining fielding contribution

## Consistency Points

Longer-term performance:

-   rolling averages
-   sustained strike rate
-   bowling economy
-   form
-   season milestones

## Development & Contribution Points

Potential future category:

-   training attendance
-   improvement milestones
-   availability
-   development goals
-   coach-approved contribution

This category must be tightly governed. Subjective coach ratings should
never quietly become equivalent to verified match statistics.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 14. SCRBRD Rewards Wallet

Each eligible player has a Rewards Wallet.

The wallet should distinguish between:

-   **Current Points** --- available reward balance.
-   **Pending Points** --- awaiting verification.
-   **Locked Points** --- temporarily unavailable.
-   **Redeemed Points** --- historically spent.
-   **Lifetime Points** --- total qualifying points earned.
-   **Tier Progress** --- progression status.

Cricket performance points should not be represented as Rand or direct
monetary value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 15. Reward Redemption Model

SCRBRD should avoid a permanent equation such as:

`100 points = R1`

That creates an uncontrolled financial liability.

Instead, points unlock controlled offers.

Example:

`1,000 SCRBRD Points → eligible for selected Tier 1 rewards`

Sponsors determine inventory, redemption limits and campaign periods.

# 22. Integration With Existing SCRBRD Modules

The Rewards Engine should consume existing SCRBRD data rather than
creating a parallel scoring system.

Primary sources include:

-   `fixtures`
-   `scoring_actions`
-   `scorecards`
-   `ball_events`
-   `player_season_stats`
-   `match_role_assignments`
-   `awards`
-   `accolades`
-   `persons`
-   `teams`
-   `seasons`

The scoring engine remains the source of truth.

The Rewards Engine consumes verified events and produces reward
transactions.

This separation is critical:

`Scoring Event → Verified Match State → Analytics → Reward Rule Evaluation → Reward Transaction → Wallet`

Rewards must **never alter the underlying cricket score**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 23. Event-Sourced Reward Processing

Because SCRBRD's scoring architecture is intended to be event sourced,
rewards should follow the same deterministic principle.

For example:

``` text
delivery recorded
      ↓
scoring event validated
      ↓
match state updated
      ↓
reward-eligible event detected
      ↓
reward rule evaluated
      ↓
pending reward transaction created
      ↓
fixture verification/finalisation
      ↓
transaction confirmed
      ↓
wallet balance updated
```

If a scoring correction changes the underlying event, the Rewards Engine
should issue an adjustment transaction.

Historical transactions should not simply be deleted.

# 24. Example

A batter scores:

**76 runs from 48 balls**

including:

-   8 fours
-   3 sixes
-   half-century
-   Player of the Match

Illustrative calculation:

``` text
Runs:
76 × 1 = 76

Four bonuses:
8 × 2 = 16

Six bonuses:
3 × 4 = 12

50 milestone:
+15

Strike-rate bonus:
+25

Player of the Match:
+40

Base Reward:
184 points
```

If the fixture qualifies for a context multiplier of 1.10:

``` text
184 × 1.10 = 202.4
```

Subject to the applicable rule configuration and rounding policy:

**Final Reward = 202 SCRBRD Points**

The UI should allow the player to see the breakdown rather than
displaying an unexplained number.

# 27. Privacy, Minors and Sponsorship

SCRBRD primarily operates in youth and school sport, making this a
critical design requirement.

Reward functionality must comply with SCRBRD's POPIA and safeguarding
architecture.

Principles include:

-   sponsor systems do not receive protected player profiles
-   medical, disciplinary and private development data cannot be used
    for commercial targeting
-   parent/guardian controls apply where required
-   redemption eligibility must be age appropriate
-   marketing consent must be separate from sporting participation
-   opting out of marketing must not prevent a player from participating
    in cricket
-   sponsor reporting should favour aggregated campaign analytics
-   personalised commercial targeting of minors should be heavily
    restricted

The rewards layer must remain logically separated from SCRBRD's
protected player data.

-   highly subjective coach contribution scores

This avoids creating false precision before SCRBRD has enough reliable
match data.

# 30. Long-Term Opportunity

SCRBRD Rewards should not be positioned merely as:

> Play cricket. Earn points. Get vouchers.

The stronger proposition is:

> **Verified performance creates value.**

The Rewards Engine connects:

**Performance → Recognition → Development → Rewards → Engagement**

As SCRBRD accumulates verified delivery-level data, the platform can
develop increasingly sophisticated models for:

-   performance benchmarking
-   player development
-   scouting
-   comparative analytics
-   competition rankings
-   sponsorship measurement
-   athlete progression

The reward system becomes an engagement layer around SCRBRD's core
asset: **verified sporting data**.
